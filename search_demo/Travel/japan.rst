Travelling in Japan
===================

Japan has one of the best railway networks in the world. The Shinkansen high speed trains connect Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka in a few hours and are almost always on time. Foreign tourists can buy a rail pass that covers most long distance trains for a fixed number of days.

Cash is still widely used, especially in small restaurants and temples, so it is useful to carry some yen. Convenience store ATMs usually accept foreign cards.

Spring, when the cherry trees bloom, and autumn, when the maple leaves turn red, are the most popular seasons. Hotels in Kyoto fill up months in advance during these periods.
