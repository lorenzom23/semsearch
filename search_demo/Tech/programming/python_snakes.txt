Pythons are large non venomous snakes found in Africa, Asia and Australia. They kill their prey by constriction: the snake wraps its body around the animal and tightens a little every time the prey breathes out, until the blood flow stops.

A reticulated python can grow longer than six metres, making it one of the longest snakes in the world. Pythons swallow their food whole thanks to a very flexible jaw, and after a big meal such as a deer or a pig they may not eat again for weeks.

Female pythons lay eggs and, unlike most snakes, coil around them to protect them. By contracting their muscles they can even raise the temperature of the eggs a few degrees, a behaviour similar to shivering in mammals.
