Apple was founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne in a garage in California. Its first product, the Apple I, was a simple circuit board sold to hobbyists. The Macintosh, launched in 1984, popularized the graphical user interface and the mouse.

After a difficult period in the nineties the company returned to growth with the iMac, the iPod and later the iPhone, introduced in 2007, which changed the mobile phone industry. Today Apple designs its own processors for phones and laptops and earns a growing share of its revenue from services such as the App Store and cloud storage.
