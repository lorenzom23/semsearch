# Spaghetti alla carbonara

Carbonara is a Roman pasta dish made with only a few ingredients: spaghetti, guanciale, eggs, Pecorino Romano and black pepper. Cream, garlic and onion are not part of the traditional recipe.

Cut the guanciale into strips and cook it slowly in a pan without oil until the fat melts and the meat becomes crispy. Meanwhile beat the egg yolks with plenty of grated pecorino and freshly ground pepper until you get a thick paste.

Cook the spaghetti in salted water and drain them a minute before they are ready, keeping a cup of the cooking water. Turn off the heat, add the pasta to the pan with the guanciale, then pour in the egg mixture and stir quickly, adding a little cooking water. The heat of the pasta should cook the eggs into a creamy sauce: if the pan is too hot they scramble and the dish is ruined.
