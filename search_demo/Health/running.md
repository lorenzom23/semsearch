# Starting to run

The most common mistake of new runners is doing too much too soon. Start by alternating one minute of slow running with two minutes of walking for twenty minutes, three times a week, and increase the running time gradually.

Run at a pace that lets you speak in full sentences. Most of your training should feel easy; speed sessions are useful only once you have built a solid base.

Good running shoes reduce the risk of injury, but there is no perfect model for everyone: choose the pair that feels comfortable in the shop. Pain in the shins or knees that lasts more than a few days is a sign to rest and reduce the weekly distance by about a third.
