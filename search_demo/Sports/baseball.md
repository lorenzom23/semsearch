# Baseball basics

Baseball is played between two teams of nine players who take turns batting and fielding. The game is divided into nine innings, and in each inning both teams get a chance to bat until three of their players are out.

The batter stands at home plate holding a wooden or aluminium bat and tries to hit the ball thrown by the pitcher. A good swing starts from the legs and hips, not from the arms: the weight moves from the back foot to the front foot while the hands stay close to the body until the last moment.

After hitting the ball the batter runs toward first base. If the ball is caught in the air before touching the ground, the batter is out. A ball hit over the outfield fence is a home run, and every runner on base scores.

Choosing the right bat matters. Younger players usually start with a light aluminium bat because it is easier to swing quickly, while professional leagues require solid wood, often maple or ash. The length and weight of the bat should match the height and strength of the player.
